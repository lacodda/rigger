# Handoff to {to} · {key}

> A text for the team that owns the fix. Ask it the way you would ask a
> colleague: what is needed and why - not how to build it, and not the paths
> of your own code. Write in the language they read; nothing here is added to
> the text but what you write. Lines that start with `>` are the form and are
> left out. When it is filled, run `rigger task handoff {key} --to {to}` again.

## Title (required)

{title}

> One line, up to about a hundred characters: what is needed.

## TL;DR (required when the text runs long)

> One to three lines a reader can stop after: what is wanted of them and why
> it matters. Needed when the text runs past fifteen lines; a short text does
> not repeat itself.

## Ask (required)

> The request, in a sentence: "Please add ...", "Please return ...".

## Now (required)

> How the system behaves now, or what is missing - what the client gets.

## Needed (required)

> What it should do instead. Name endpoints, fields and error codes when they
> are known; mark what is not known as "to be agreed" rather than invent it.

## Why (required)

> What it gives the user, which scenario it closes - "Why: ...".

## Example (optional)

> A request, a response, a field - only if the ask cannot do without one.
> Paste it as a fenced code block.
