## Where the work is done (required)

> Full paths to the directories that may be changed, one per line. Several
> copies of a project are often checked out, and a folder's name does not say
> which branch it holds - without this, nothing is touched.
> Example:
> C:\work\webapp-release\client\
> C:\work\api\ (read only, to check the API against)

## The task as written (required)

> The ticket's text, pasted as it is. Do not retell it: the customer's wording
> is often the hint.

## What was reproduced (required for a bug)

> Five lines. A screenshot shows what happened, never what should have. The
> time is what finds the right place in a log of thirty megabytes.
>
> - Did:
> - Got:
> - Expected:
> - Version and stand:
> - Time:

## Links to files (optional)

> Folders a tester left material in, one per line: `rigger tray fetch` copies
> each of them here. Recordings are listed, not copied - if the point is on a
> recording, say what it shows in two lines.
> Example:
> \\fileserver\testers\2026\0915\3

## Done before (optional)

> Commits, branches, words to search the history for: where earlier attempts
> are. When this is filled, it saves half a sitting.

## Compare against (optional)

> The reference: where to see how it should be - another client, a sibling
> product, a protocol, an API schema. Without it, nobody knows what a complete
> list is.

## From colleagues (optional)

> Hints, snippets and answers from neighbouring teams, quoted, with who said
> them. Kept apart from the task: this is a version, not a requirement.

## What the files show (optional)

> A line per file: what it is for. With two or three files this is not needed;
> with five similar screenshots it saves both time and a wrong pick.
